Compare before committing

Your JoyaGoo size guide starts with measurements.

Choose one well-fitting item as your reference. Record how it was measured so your comparisons remain consistent across sellers and categories.

A practical reference for sneaker sizing, clothing measurements and accessory fit.
Category What to compare What to resolve before ordering
Shoes & sneakers Foot length, the model's shoe size chart, toe-box shape and outsole photos. Confirm the exact colorway and size system. A familiar size number is only a starting point.
Hoodies & sweaters Chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and fabric composition. Check whether an oversized fit comes from extra width, dropped shoulders or added body length.
T-shirts & jerseys Body length, neckline, sleeve opening and print placement. Confirm whether measurements describe the finished garment or the intended body size.
Jackets & sets Layering room, lining details, zipper construction and the measurements of each piece. For matching sets, check the top and bottoms separately rather than assuming one size fits both.
Pants, shorts & underwear Waist measurement, rise, inseam length, stretch and material description. Check whether the waist was measured relaxed or stretched and read the relevant return conditions.
Headwear & accessories Cap circumference, strap adjustment, bracelet dimensions and fastening details. Ask for any missing measurement or close-up image before relying on a visual estimate.

From discovery to a decision

A practical JoyaGoo shopping guide.

Keep a short, useful record for each candidate. A source link, exact variation and a few measurements are more valuable than a long list of unchecked bookmarks.

Budget the whole order. Separate the item price from currency conversion, any applicable service charges, domestic delivery, international shipping and destination charges. Confirm current totals and delivery options with the provider you use.

  1. Keep JoyaGoo product links traceable.

    Save the original product URL, seller name and item ID alongside your notes. For Weidian links, Taobao listings or 1688 product references, retain the source identifier so an expired bookmark is easier to investigate.

  2. Compare the exact variation.

    Record the selected color, size and material. Check product availability on the current listing. A similar thumbnail may show a different version, seller or construction.

  3. Distinguish listing images from JoyaGoo QC photos.

    Seller images introduce a design. Warehouse photos, when provided for your order, can help you inspect the received item. Check size labels, stitching details, color consistency, visible marks and whether the selected pieces are present.

  4. Resolve gaps before the next payment.

    Request a missing measurement or clearer detail photo where the service permits it. Review applicable seller terms and shipping estimates before continuing. Photos alone do not establish authenticity or guarantee fit.

Answers, without the guesswork

JoyaGoo spreadsheet FAQ.

What this guide covers, where its links lead and which details still need checking on a live listing.

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

The term usually describes a community list of product references intended for use with JoyaGoo. A useful list helps you compare categories, source links, options and available photos. This page is a guide and a directory of CAFANS category links, rather than a live spreadsheet.

Where do the links on this page go?

The ten category links open their corresponding categories on cafans.com. All other navigation links open the CAFANS homepage at https://www.cafans.com. This page does not send orders to JoyaGoo.

Are the photographs JoyaGoo QC photos?

No. These are brand product images used as visual references for shoes, clothing, headwear and jewelry. They do not show a purchased item, establish authenticity or confirm availability on CAFANS.

How should I choose a clothing or shoe size?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item that already fits you. For clothing, check chest width, length and sleeve length. For shoes, compare the relevant foot-length guidance and the exact model's size chart. Do not rely on a size label alone.

Does a spreadsheet price include the full order cost?

Do not assume that it does. Check the current item price, currency conversion, any applicable service charges, domestic delivery, international shipping and destination charges before paying. This guide provides no price or delivery quotation.

What should I do with an expired product link?

Keep the original source URL and item ID, then search for that exact reference in the relevant catalogue. Compare the seller, photos, options and measurements before treating another listing as a match.

Is this an official JoyaGoo or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide linking to CAFANS. It is not operated by, endorsed by or officially affiliated with JoyaGoo, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
